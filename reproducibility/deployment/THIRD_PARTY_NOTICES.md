# Third-party software and data

This repository does not grant rights to third-party model code, weights or datasets. Upstream software remains subject to its own terms.

BEDICT-V2 and FORECasT-BE have local MIT-license evidence. Relevant DeepBaseEditor subdirectories also contain MIT notices, but the entire paired-library bundle is not assumed to share one license. BE-Hive, Yachie, BE_Endo, DeepBE and EPCNNBE lack sufficient model-level redistribution evidence in the local audit. CRISPRon-BE includes Business Source License 1.1 and component-license constraints.

The repository therefore distributes first-party adapters, environment descriptions and upstream manifests—not copied third-party repositories or weights.
